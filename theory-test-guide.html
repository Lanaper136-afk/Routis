UK driving theory test: how it works

The car theory test has two parts, taken one after the other on the same day. Here is what to expect and how to prepare.

Part 1: multiple choice

  • 50 questions, each with several answers to choose from.
  • You have 57 minutes.
  • The pass mark is 43 out of 50.

Part 2: hazard perception

  • You watch 14 video clips. They contain 15 developing hazards.
  • You click as soon as you see a hazard start to develop. The earlier you click, the more you score, up to 5 points for each hazard.
  • The pass mark is 44 out of 75.
  • Do not click constantly or in a pattern: you can score zero for that clip.

After you pass

  • Your pass certificate is valid for two years. You must pass your practical test within that time.
  • You must pass both parts in the same sitting. If you fail one part you take the whole test again.

How to prepare

  • Read the study sheets for the key Highway Code rules.
  • Learn the road signs and test yourself with the sign quiz.
  • Take a 50-question mock test, then review every answer you got wrong.
  • Practise spotting hazards with the hazard practice scenes. They are not the official clips.
