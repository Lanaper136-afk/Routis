United Kingdom

UK mandatory and information signs

Blue circles tell you what you must do. Blue and white rectangles give you information.

19 signs

Ahead only

You must go straight ahead.

Do not turn left or right.

Turn left ahead

You must turn left at the junction ahead.

Prepare early and signal.

Turn right ahead

You must turn right at the junction ahead.

Prepare early and signal.

Keep left

You must keep to the left of the obstacle.

The arrow shows which side to pass.

Vehicles may pass either side

You may pass on either side of the obstacle to reach the same destination.

Take care and watch other traffic.

Mini-roundabout

Give way to traffic from the immediate right at the mini-roundabout.

Drive round the central marking, not over it, when you can.

Route for pedal cycles only

Cycle route: pedal cycles only. Other vehicles must not use it.

Do not park or drive in a cycle lane.

Minimum speed

You must drive at least at the speed shown (30 mph here), unless it is unsafe.

Does not require you to break other speed limits.

One-way traffic

Traffic may go only in the direction of the arrow.

Check for oncoming vehicles when turning into one-way streets.

Parking

A place where you may park.

Check any time limits and payment rules.

Hospital ahead

Hospital ahead with Accident and Emergency facilities.

Give way to ambulances.

Bus lane

A bus lane: for buses (and sometimes cycles and taxis), at the times shown.

Do not drive in a bus lane during its hours.

Priority over oncoming vehicles

You have priority over vehicles coming from the opposite direction.

Still take care at narrow points.

Recommended route for pedal cycles

A recommended route for pedal cycles.

Watch for cyclists and give them space.

Countdown markers

Markers before a motorway exit: each bar shows about 100 yards to the exit.

Move into the left lane in good time to leave.

Start of motorway

Start of the motorway: motorway regulations apply from here.

Learners cannot use motorways except with an approved instructor in dual-control cars.

End of motorway

End of the motorway: motorway regulations no longer apply.

Check the new speed limit as you leave.

No through road

No through road for vehicles.

You will have to turn round at the end.

Keep right

You must keep to the right of the obstacle.

The arrow shows which side to pass.
