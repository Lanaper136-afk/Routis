UK warning signs

Warning signs are mostly red-bordered triangles. They warn of a hazard ahead so you can slow down and prepare.

39 warning signs

Crossroads

A crossroads is ahead.

Look out for traffic from both sides.

T-junction

A T-junction is ahead with priority over vehicles from the right.

Watch for vehicles joining.

Side road on the left

A side road joins on the left ahead.

Look out for emerging vehicles.

Side road on the right

A side road joins on the right ahead.

Look out for emerging vehicles.

Staggered junction

A staggered junction is ahead.

Traffic may cross or turn in front of you.

Roundabout

A roundabout is ahead.

Give way to traffic from the right and signal as needed.

Traffic merging from left

Traffic is merging from the left ahead.

Make room for merging vehicles where possible.

Bend to right

A bend to the right is ahead.

Slow down before the bend.

Bend to left

A bend to the left is ahead.

Slow down before the bend.

Double bend

Double bend, first to the left (the symbol may be reversed).

Do not overtake and keep your speed down.

Road narrows on both sides

The road narrows on both sides ahead.

Be ready to give way to oncoming traffic.

Road narrows on right

The road narrows on the right ahead (left if the symbol is reversed).

Watch for oncoming traffic.

Dual carriageway ends

The dual carriageway ends ahead and becomes two-way traffic.

Expect oncoming traffic and no central barrier.

Uneven road

The road surface is uneven ahead.

Slow down to keep control.

Hump bridge

A hump bridge is ahead. You may not be able to see over the top.

Slow down and keep to your side.

Slippery road

The road may be slippery.

Reduce speed and brake gently.

Tunnel ahead

There is a tunnel ahead.

Switch on dipped headlights if needed.

Cycle route ahead

A cycle route crosses or joins the road ahead.

Watch for cyclists.

Traffic queues likely

Queues are likely ahead.

Slow down in good time and use your hazard lights if needed.

Other danger

Other danger: the plate below tells you what the danger is.

Read the plate.

Side winds

Strong side winds are possible.

Hold the steering wheel firmly, especially on bridges and open roads.

Low-flying aircraft

Low-flying aircraft or sudden aircraft noise.

Keep control of your vehicle.

Traffic signals ahead

Traffic signals are ahead.

Be ready to stop on red.

Trams crossing ahead

Trams cross the road ahead.

Trams cannot swerve: give them room.

Loose chippings

Loose chippings on the road surface.

Keep your speed down and avoid braking hard.

School crossing patrol ahead

A school crossing patrol is ahead (some signs have amber lights that flash when the crossing is in use).

Stop when the patrol steps into the road with the sign.

Zebra crossing

A zebra crossing is ahead.

Give way to anyone on or stepping onto the crossing.

Wild animals

Wild animals may be on the road ahead.

Reduce speed, especially at dusk and dawn.

Steep hill downwards

A steep descent ahead (the percentage shows the gradient).

Use a low gear to help control speed.

Steep hill upwards

A steep ascent ahead (the percentage shows the gradient).

Select a suitable gear in advance.

Level crossing with barrier

A level crossing with a barrier or gate is ahead.

Never drive onto a crossing unless the road beyond is clear.

Level crossing without barrier

A level crossing without a barrier or gate is ahead.

Look and listen for trains.

Risk of ice

There is a risk of ice on the road.

Increase your distance and avoid sudden moves.

Falling or fallen rocks

Rocks may fall or lie on the road ahead.

Look out for debris and avoid stopping under cliffs.

Road works

Road works ahead.

Slow down and follow the signs.

Road narrows on left

The road narrows on the left ahead.

Watch for oncoming traffic and keep to your side.

Traffic merging from right

Traffic is merging from the right ahead.

Make room for merging vehicles where possible.

Two-way traffic

Two-way traffic is ahead, on a road that was one-way.

Keep to the left and expect oncoming vehicles.

Road humps

Road humps (speed humps) are ahead.

Slow down to the speed shown for the humps.
