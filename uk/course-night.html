Night driving, fog and tiredness

Rules 91, 113–116, 226–237

  • Headlights from sunset to sunrise and whenever you cannot see clearly (generally when visibility is under 100 metres).
  • Dip your headlights for oncoming traffic and when following another vehicle. Full beam can dazzle.
  • Front fog lights: only when visibility is seriously reduced. Switch them off when it improves.
  • Dazzled? Slow down and stop if you have to.
  • Tiredness: plan a break of at least 15 minutes after every 2 hours of driving. If you feel sleepy, stop somewhere safe. Fresh air and the radio only help for a very short time.
