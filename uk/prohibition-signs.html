UK prohibition and order signs

Order signs are mostly red-bordered circles. They tell you what you must not do.

25 prohibition signs

Maximum speed 20 mph

You must not drive faster than 20 miles per hour.

Common outside schools and in residential streets.

Maximum speed 30 mph

You must not drive faster than 30 miles per hour.

The usual limit in built-up areas with street lights.

Maximum speed 40 mph

You must not drive faster than 40 miles per hour.

Often used on busier town roads.

Maximum speed 50 mph

You must not drive faster than 50 miles per hour.

Common on faster urban roads.

Maximum speed 60 mph

You must not drive faster than 60 miles per hour.

Seen on some single carriageway roads.

Maximum speed 70 mph

You must not drive faster than 70 miles per hour.

Typical on dual carriageways and some single roads.

National speed limit applies

The national speed limit applies here. The limit depends on the road and your vehicle: for a car, 60 mph on a single carriageway and 70 mph on a dual carriageway or motorway.

This is not a speed in itself: check which road you are on and your vehicle type.

Stop

You must stop at the line and give way to traffic on the major road before moving off.

Stop completely, even if the road looks clear.

Give way

Give way to traffic on the major road. You do not have to stop if the road is clear.

Be ready to stop if you need to.

No entry

No entry for vehicular traffic.

Often found at the exit of one-way streets.

No vehicles

No vehicles of any kind (including bicycles, which must be pushed).

A pedestrian-only street may be signed this way.

No motor vehicles

No motor vehicles. Pedal cycles and horses are allowed.

Check the sign: a car and a motorcycle are shown.

No cycling

Cycling is not allowed.

Pushing the bicycle is usually permitted.

No buses

No buses (over 8 passenger seats).

Does not apply to coaches or buses unless the sign says so.

No overtaking

You must not overtake. The red car on the sign is the one being overtaken.

Applies until you see the end of restriction sign.

No right turn

You must not turn right.

Look for another route.

No left turn

You must not turn left.

Look for another route.

No U-turns

You must not make a U-turn.

You can still go round a roundabout.

Weight limit

No vehicles over the maximum gross weight shown (here 7.5 tonnes).

Weight is in tonnes. Local deliveries may be allowed: read plates.

Width limit

No vehicles over the width shown.

Check the width of your vehicle, including mirrors if needed.

Height limit

No vehicles over the height shown.

Tall vehicles and loads must check this before entering.

Give priority to vehicles from opposite direction

You must give way to oncoming vehicles: the red arrow is your traffic, the black arrow has priority.

Used where the road narrows.

No waiting

No waiting during the times shown on the plate.

You may stop briefly to pick up or set down passengers.

No stopping (Clearway)

You must not stop on the carriageway or the verge, except in an emergency.

A red cross on blue: no stopping at all.

No pedestrians

Pedestrians are not allowed on this road.

Seen on motorways and some bridges and tunnels.
