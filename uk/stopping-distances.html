Stopping distances

Your stopping distance is your thinking distance plus your braking distance. It grows much faster than your speed.

Typical stopping distances

SpeedThinking distanceBraking distanceOverall stopping distance
20 mph6 m6 m12 m
30 mph9 m14 m23 m
40 mph12 m24 m36 m
50 mph15 m38 m53 m
60 mph18 m55 m73 m
70 mph21 m75 m96 m

Typical stopping distances in dry conditions, a driver in good condition and a vehicle in good order.

Rules of thumb

  • On high-speed roads leave at least a two-second gap from the vehicle in front.
  • In the wet, leave at least double the gap. On ice, the distance can be up to ten times greater.
  • Large vehicles and motorcycles can need more room to stop.
  • Thinking distance gets longer if you are tired, distracted or have taken alcohol or drugs.
