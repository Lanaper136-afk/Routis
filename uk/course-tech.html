Technology, e-scooters and low emission zones

Rules 150–151 and GOV.UK

  • Driver assistance (lane-keeping, adaptive cruise control) does not replace you. You stay responsible and must stay alert.
  • Avoid cruise control on twisting roads, in heavy traffic and on slippery roads.
  • Private e-scooters are illegal on public roads, pavements and cycle lanes. Only rental e-scooters in trial areas are legal, for riders aged 16 or over with a driving licence.
  • Low emission zones (for example ULEZ in London) limit or charge vehicles that do not meet emission standards.
  • Electric vehicles are quiet. Take extra care near pedestrians.
