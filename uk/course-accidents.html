Accidents and emergencies

Rules 281–286 and 275–277

  • At the scene: warn other traffic, switch off all engines and stop smoking. Call 999 or 112.
  • Casualties: do not move them unless there is immediate danger such as fire. Do not remove a motorcyclist's helmet unless it is essential.
  • Your duties: stop and give your details. If you cannot, report it to the police as soon as possible and within 24 hours.
  • Breakdown on a motorway: leave by the left-hand door, wait behind the barrier and use the roadside phone. Never use a warning triangle on a motorway.
