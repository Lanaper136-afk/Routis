UK speed limits

The national speed limits depend on the road and on the type of vehicle. They are a maximum, not a target.

Speed limits table (England, Scotland and Wales)

VehicleBuilt-upSingle carriagewayDual carriagewayMotorway
Cars and motorcycles30 (20 in Wales)607070
Cars towing a caravan or trailer30 (20 in Wales)506060
Buses, coaches and minibuses (up to 12 m)30 (20 in Wales)506070
Goods vehicles up to 7.5 tonnes30 (20 in Wales)506070 (60 if articulated or towing)
Goods vehicles over 7.5 tonnes, England and Wales30 (20 in Wales)506060
Goods vehicles over 7.5 tonnes, Scotland30405060

Maximum speeds in mph, from Rule 124 (selection of vehicle types). Buses over 12 m long are limited to 60 mph on motorways.

Things to remember

  • In built-up areas with street lights the limit is usually 30 mph, unless signs show otherwise. Wales has a default 20 mph limit on restricted roads.
  • The national speed limit sign is a white circle with a black diagonal stripe.
  • The limit is the absolute maximum. You must slow down for bends, weather, pedestrians and heavy traffic.
  • A speed limit in a red ring on a motorway gantry is mandatory and can be enforced by cameras.
